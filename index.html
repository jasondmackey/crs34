Payload Highlights
Key Science Payloads

CRS-34 emphasizes microgravity research with experiments like ODYSSEY, which tests Earth-based simulators against real space conditions by observing bacterial behavior. Green Bone grows bone cells on a rattan wood scaffold to study osteoporosis treatments in microgravity, while SPARK analyzes red blood cell and spleen changes for long-duration mission health safeguards. Additional payloads include STORIE for monitoring space weather-impacted charged particles (protecting grids/satellites), Laplace for dust particle dynamics in planet formation, and CLARREO Pathfinder for Earth/Moon sunlight reflection measurements.

Hardware and Supplies
SpaceX Dragon spacecraft

Cargo supports ISS operations with a European exercise device power cable, catalytic reactor for wastewater processing, two universal pretreat tanks (plus adapter) for waste systems, ARMADILLO cartridges for water recovery, nitrogen tank, Ultraprobe, remote sensor unit, and hull repair patches. These spares ensure redundancy in life support and maintenance, critical for continuous habitation. NASA astronaut Jack Hathaway and ESA's Sophie Adenot will oversee docking.

Featured Payload
CLARREO Pathfinder

CLARREO Pathfinder (Calibration Absolute Radiance and Refractivity Observatory Pathfinder) demonstrates an ability to take highly accurate measurements of sunlight reflected by Earth and the Moon. These measurements, which are anchored to international standards, will be five to ten times more accurate than those from existing sensors.

CLARREO Pathfinder is designed to have the unique ability to maintain its high accuracy throughout its lifetime — showcasing technology that can be of use to all providers of Earth observations. Higher accuracy means greater certainty in measurements, enabling CLARREO Pathfinder to provide a calibration standard that could improve government and commercial fleets.
